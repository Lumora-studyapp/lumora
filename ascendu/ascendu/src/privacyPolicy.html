Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

Lumora is a study and focus application. This Privacy Policy explains what information Lumora collects, why it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

We collect information needed to create your account and provide Lumora's features.

  • Account information: your email address, username, account identifier, and authentication-provider details.
  • Social sign-in information: if you choose Google or Apple sign-in, we receive the basic account information that provider makes available, such as your email address, name, or provider account identifier. Lumora does not receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Study and progress information: study sessions, subjects, goals, checklists, assessments, study totals, streaks, coins, badges, cosmetic selections, and related progress records.
  • Community information: your username, friends, groups, leaderboard results, and study-presence information used for Lumora's social features.
  • Preferences: settings and choices used to personalise your Lumora experience.
  • Support information: information you include when you contact us for help or make a privacy request.
  • Technical information: Firebase and the services used to operate Lumora may process limited technical records such as IP address, device or browser type, operating system, timestamps, authentication events, and error or security logs.

Lumora does not collect payment-card details, billing addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, calendar contents, biometrics, or call recordings.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, secure, and maintain your account;
  • save and synchronise your study activity and preferences;
  • calculate progress, streaks, rewards, achievements, and leaderboard positions;
  • provide friends, groups, and study-presence features you choose to use;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • detect abuse, troubleshoot problems, and protect Lumora and its users; and
  • comply with applicable laws and enforce our rights.

Lumora does not use your information for targeted advertising, third-party advertising, offer walls, or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Authentication and service providers

Lumora uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to authenticate users and store application data. Google processes information on our behalf under its applicable service terms and privacy commitments.

If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that provider processes your sign-in according to its own privacy policy. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate, secure, and support Lumora, or when required by law.

4. Information visible to other users

When you use Lumora's community features, other Lumora users may be able to see information needed for those features, such as your username, study totals, leaderboard position, shared group information, and study-presence status. Do not use a username that reveals information you do not want other users to see.

5. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Lumora does not sell personal information and does not use advertising networks, targeted advertising, offer walls, Google Analytics advertising features, or cross-app tracking. Lumora and its service providers may use essential local storage or similar technology where needed for sign-in, security, preferences, and core application functions.

6. Data retention

We retain account and study information while your Lumora account is active so we can provide the service. If your account is deleted, we remove or anonymise associated information from active systems unless limited retention is reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or compliance with law. Residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups until those backups are overwritten through the service provider's normal cycle.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access and provider security controls. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

Lumora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If local law requires parental or guardian consent for a younger user, that user must not create an account without the required consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to this section, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

9. International processing

Lumora is operated from Australia. Our service providers may process or store information in Australia, the United States, or other countries where they operate. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect information handled on our behalf.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, receive a copy of, object to certain processing of, or request deletion of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. Some requests may be limited where retention is required by law or necessary to protect Lumora and its users.

To make a privacy request, email lumora.studyapp@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request relates to your account.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Lumora's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The date at the top will be updated when a new version takes effect. Material changes may also be communicated within the app or through the contact details associated with your account.
